Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 August 2026

This policy covers indiandeals2buy.com. In short: I collect nothing about you directly, but the site does carry Google ads and Amazon affiliate links, and both of those set cookies. The detail is below.

The short version

  • There are no accounts, no logins and no forms here, so the site never asks you for personal information.
  • I do not sell, rent or share personal information, because I do not hold any.
  • Third parties — Google for advertising, Amazon for affiliate links — do set cookies and collect data about your visit. You can opt out of the advertising ones; links are below.

Information I collect directly

None. These are static pages: there is no database, no account system, no newsletter and no analytics package running on this site. I cannot see who visits or what they look at.

If you email me, I necessarily have your address and whatever you chose to put in the message. I use it to reply to you and nothing else — it does not go on a list and it is not passed to anyone.

Hosting and server logs

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub records technical details of each request — IP address, browser user agent, page requested, time — to run the service and prevent abuse. I have no access to those logs. Their handling is governed by GitHub's own privacy statement.

Advertising cookies

Ads on this site are served by Google. Specifically:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the internet.
  • You can opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings.
  • You can opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info/choices.
  • Google explains what it collects from sites that use its services in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Opting out of personalised ads does not remove ads — it means the ones you see are chosen without using your browsing history.

Amazon affiliate links

This site participates in the Amazon Associates Programme. Links to Amazon carry a tracking tag, and Amazon sets a cookie when you follow one so that any resulting purchase can be attributed to this site and a commission paid.

What I receive from Amazon is anonymous and aggregated: item counts and commission totals. I never see your name, address, payment details or what you ordered. Once you land on amazon.in you are on Amazon's site and Amazon's own privacy notice applies.

Visitors in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland

This site is aimed at shoppers in India, but it is reachable from anywhere. Google is required to obtain consent before using cookies for personalised advertising for visitors in those regions, and where that applies you may see a consent prompt served by Google before ads load. Whatever you choose there can be changed later through the Google Ads Settings link above, and you can clear the site's cookies in your browser at any time.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your choices

  • Block or delete cookies in your browser settings — nothing here breaks without them.
  • Opt out of personalised advertising using the links above.
  • Use an ad blocker. The site works perfectly well with one, and I would rather you read the deals than see the ads.

Changes to this policy

If the site starts doing something new with data — adding analytics, say — this page gets updated and the date at the top changes with it. There is no mailing list to notify, so the date is how you tell.
