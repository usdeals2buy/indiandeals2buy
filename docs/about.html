About

IndianDeals2Buy is a hand-curated shortlist of deals from India's online stores. No infinite feed, no clickbait, no fake countdown timers — just products worth a look, each linking straight to the store that sells it.

What this site is

Every product here was added by hand, one at a time. The list stays deliberately small: it is easier to trust twenty products someone actually chose than twenty thousand pulled in by a script. Deals are grouped by category and the whole catalogue is searchable from the front page. There is nothing to sign up for and nothing to buy on this site itself — every link takes you to the retailer to complete the purchase there.

Today those links all point to Amazon India, which is where most of the deals worth listing currently are. Other Indian retailers will be added as coverage grows, and each listing will always say plainly where it sends you before you click.

What this site is not

These are not hands-on reviews. I do not receive products from brands, and I have not personally tested most of what is listed. Descriptions and specifications come from the retailer's own product listing. Treat this as a starting point rather than a verdict — read the reviews and the seller details on the store before you buy anything.

About the prices

Retailers change prices constantly. Any price shown here was accurate when the product was added and may well be wrong by the time you read it. The price at checkout is the only one that counts. If you spot a listing that has drifted badly out of date, tell me and I will fix or remove it.

How this site makes money

Two ways, both disclosed openly:

  • Affiliate commissions. Links to stores carry an affiliate tag — currently through the Amazon Associates Programme, with other retailer programmes to follow. If you buy something after clicking one, the retailer pays me a small commission. You pay exactly the same price you would have paid otherwise; the commission comes out of the retailer's margin, not your pocket.
  • Advertising. Pages carry ads served by Google. I do not choose the individual ads, and an advertiser cannot buy their way onto the deal list.

Neither of those decides what gets listed. A product earns its place because it looks like good value, not because it pays better. Where I have doubts about a product, it does not go up.

Get in touch

Corrections, broken links, suggestions or removal requests are all welcome — the contact page has the details. If you want to know what happens to your data when you visit, that is covered in the privacy policy.
