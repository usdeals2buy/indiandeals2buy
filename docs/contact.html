Worth writing about

  • Price or specification corrections. Retail prices move constantly and listings get edited. If something here is out of date, point me at it and I will fix or pull it.
  • Broken or wrong links. A link that 404s, or lands on a different product than the one described.
  • Removal requests. If you are a brand, seller or rights holder and want a listing taken down, say which page and why. I would rather remove something quickly than argue about it.
  • Suggestions. A product you think belongs here, or a category the site is missing.
  • Business enquiries. Advertising, partnerships and anything commercial.

What I cannot help with

I do not sell anything, and I have no access to your account with any retailer, your orders or your payment details. For order status, delivery problems, returns or refunds you need the customer service of the store you bought from — Amazon India for everything currently listed here. Buying through a link on this site gives me no visibility into the purchase, so I cannot look it up or intervene.

Response time

I read everything that arrives and usually reply within a few days. Corrections and takedown requests jump the queue. If a week goes by with no answer, it is safe to assume the message went astray — send it again.
